Become a Nursing Cyber Fellow

A paid, part-time program for nursing undergraduates who want to understand the technology behind modern patient care, and how to keep it safe.

Apply to Become a Nursing Cyber Fellow

A joint cohort of 15 students

10 from VCU and 5 from other Virginia institutions with nursing programs.

$3,000
stipend per Fellow

  • No technical background requiredBuilt for nursing students. No coding or cybersecurity experience is expected.
  • Fits around clinicalsShort asynchronous lessons you complete on your own schedule.
  • Nursing and Computer ScienceLearn from both perspectives, with materials developed jointly by faculty in each.
  • Hands-on assignmentsWork with devices or device data from the Smart Healthcare Security Lab.
  • Talks from practitionersPossible talks by people working in healthcare cybersecurity, nursing informatics, and clinical AI.
  • Path to an externshipShort externship opportunities with healthcare partners in summer 2027. More information will be shared with Fellows later in the program.

What you will learn

Three modules, developed jointly by Nursing and Computer Science faculty. Each combines 5–10 minute video lessons, quizzes, and practical assignments.

Module 1

AI Tools in Healthcare

Where AI already appears in nursing work and how it fits into clinical workflows.

  • Wearables and remote monitoring
  • Predictive analytics and decision support
  • Virtual assistants and robotics
Module 2

Healthcare Data, Analytics, and Privacy

How health data are collected, cleaned, and used, and the rules that protect them.

  • EHR systems and sensor data
  • Basic data analysis and visualization
  • HIPAA, patient privacy, ethical AI use
Module 3

Cybersecurity in AI-Driven Healthcare

How connected clinical systems fail or are attacked, and what nurses can do about it.

  • Medical device and wireless risks
  • AI bias and manipulated inputs
  • Access control, HIPAA Security Rule, NIST CSF

Questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The program is designed for nursing students and taught from a clinical point of view. You may get to try some basic data analysis using AI prompts, but no prior experience is needed.

I'm at a community college. Can I apply?

Yes. Five seats are reserved for students from other Virginia institutions with nursing programs.

How much time does it take?

The program is online and self-paced, with short lessons and assignments posted weekly so they fit around clinical schedules. There may also be a few talks during the spring semester. A detailed schedule will be shared with selected Fellows.

Do I have to visit the lab first?

Fellows are expected to have attended a lab session.

What you will learn

Three connected learning modules

Module 01

AI Tools in Healthcare

Explore AI applications in nursing, including patient monitoring, decision support, and clinical workflows.

Module 02

Healthcare Data, Analytics & Privacy

Understand EHRs, sensor data, visualizations, responsible data handling, and patient privacy.

Module 03

Cybersecurity in AI-Driven Healthcare

Examine connected-device risks, access control, AI reliability, and secure use of healthcare systems.

Fellowship experience

Learn with mentors. Apply what you learn.

The proposed program includes short online learning activities, practical assignments, guest seminars, and regular check-ins with Nursing and Computer Science mentors.

Up to 15 nursing undergraduates are planned to participate, including students from additional CCI institutions. The project budget includes a $3,000 stipend per Fellow, subject to final program and eligibility details.

No prior programming or cybersecurity experience is required for the introductory lab. Fellowship selection considers academic readiness, interest, and engagement.

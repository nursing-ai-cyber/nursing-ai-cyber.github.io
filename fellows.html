Nursing Cyber Fellows

A deeper experiential pathway for nursing students who want to understand how AI, connected technologies, data privacy, and cybersecurity are changing healthcare.

Go beyond the lab visit

Up to 15 undergraduate nursing students will be selected for structured training, mentoring, practical assignments, and interaction with experts in healthcare, informatics, AI, and cybersecurity.

No technical background is required.
The program is designed to make technology-focused healthcare topics accessible to nursing students with strong clinical curiosity.
Training modules

Three connected areas

AI Tools in Healthcare

Wearables, predictive analytics, virtual assistants, automation, clinical decision support, and how AI fits into nursing workflows.

Healthcare Data, Analytics & Privacy

Sensor data, EHR concepts, data quality, visualization, privacy, HIPAA, and responsible use of health information.

Cybersecurity in AI-Driven Healthcare

Connected-device attack surfaces, wireless communication, access control, AI limitations, safe use, and system integrity.

Early summer 2027

From learning to clinical practice

Fellows are planned to complete short mentored externships at VCU Health and, where possible, other regional healthcare organizations. The goal is to connect the training to real clinical workflows, health IT, nursing informatics, and cybersecurity practice.

Planned cohort

Up to 15 Fellows
10 from VCU and up to 5 from partner CCI institutions.

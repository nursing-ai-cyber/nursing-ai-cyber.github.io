About the project

Preparing a cyber-aware nursing workforce

An interdisciplinary Virginia Commonwealth University initiative connecting nursing education with artificial intelligence, digital health, privacy, and cybersecurity.

Project vision

The program combines a Smart Healthcare Security Lab, the Nursing Cyber Fellows program, and planned healthcare externships. It is designed to help nursing students understand both the opportunities of emerging healthcare technologies and the risks that come with connected and AI-enabled systems.

The project is supported by the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative (CCI) Experiential Learning Program 2026.

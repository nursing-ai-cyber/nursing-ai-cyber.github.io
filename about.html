About the project

Building a cyber-aware nursing workforce

A collaboration between Virginia Commonwealth University’s Department of Computer Science and School of Nursing, supported by the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative.

Our mission

Clinical judgment meets connected technology

As healthcare systems increasingly rely on AI and connected devices, nursing students need opportunities to understand both the benefits and the risks. This experiential learning project combines hands-on lab activities, structured Fellows training, and planned healthcare externships.

The project aims to make cybersecurity and privacy concepts approachable for students whose primary focus is patient care.

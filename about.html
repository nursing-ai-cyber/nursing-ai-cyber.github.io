About the project

Preparing nurses to use AI-enabled and connected healthcare technology safely, with an eye on privacy, security, and patient safety.

Mission

AI tools and connected devices are arriving in clinical care faster than training on how to use them safely. Nurses sit at the point where patients, devices, and data meet, which makes them an important and often overlooked line of defense.

This project aims to give nursing students hands-on experience with these technologies and the habits to question them: what a device collects, where its data travel, and when its output deserves a second look.

A Nursing and Computer Science collaboration

The program is a partnership between the VCU College of Engineering's Department of Computer Science and the VCU School of Nursing. Nursing faculty bring the clinical context; Computer Science faculty and doctoral students bring expertise in AI, mobile sensing, and security. Students learn from both.

The project is supported by the Commonwealth Cyber Initiative (CCI) Experiential Learning Program, which funds hands-on learning that builds Virginia's cybersecurity workforce. VCU Health is the primary externship partner.
